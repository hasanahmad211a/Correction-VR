A guide to the evidence

How to read this field report

Follow the selected year from fire activity through air and water indicators to fishery outcomes. Coverage varies by country, basin, and measure.

01 / EXPLORE

Start with place and year

Choose a country, then switch to its case-study basin. Scrub the timeline or press play to follow mapped fire activity and the available measurements.

02 / COMPARE

Read patterns, not proof

Trend lines, before-and-after comparisons, and lagged correlations summarize patterns in the selected data. They describe association; they do not establish that fire caused a downstream change.

03 / KEEP IN MIND

Satellite indicators have limits

Aerosol optical depth is a column measurement, not a direct reading of ground-level exposure. Satellite water observations and fishery records also have different coverage and time scales.
